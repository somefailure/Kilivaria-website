About Kilivaria

Kilivaria is an old nation from Eldham. Founded in may 2023 as a simple arti-nation Kilivaria has taken off into a formidable kingdom going from a pile of garbage out sea to being mentioned as a focal point in stoney's own streams from time to time. Kilivaria is known for having indepth lore, art and creatives, the biggest part of Kilivaria is it's religion of Kryhal. Kryhal's the most indepth religion on the entire server with a wikipage of 150K+ (at the time of counting) bytes and actively being expanded with around 100 followers across multiple continents.
Modern Kilivaria can be found mainly in Talasi and Iristoya with it's capitol Wot’san’teh’zu being named in the sister language of Kilivarian, Deancherloussian meaning "New holy sand city".
